# P80 — month-end London (WMR) fix hedging flow (real data, 2010-2025)

_FX: EURUSD 2010-01-03→2025-12-31, GBPUSD 2010-01-03→2025-12-31, NZDUSD 2010-01-03→2025-12-31. Equity indices: US=^GSPC, EURUSD=^STOXX50E, GBPUSD=^FTSE, NZDUSD=^NZ50. Month-end observations 562, placebo 567._

Prediction made BEFORE the fix: foreign stocks beat the S&P 500 this month → that currency is sold into the 16:00 London fix (pair DOWN); S&P won → dollar sold (pair UP). FOLLOW = pair move in the 4h before the fix in the predicted direction (bp). Placebo = same method two weeks earlier.

## The verdict — all three pairs pooled

| half | month-ends | FOLLOW bp (t) | hit % | placebo FOLLOW bp (t) | beats placebo | 2h after the fix bp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2010-17 | 288 | -1.60 (-0.7) | 49% | +0.30 (+0.2) | no | -2.44 (-1.6) |
| 2018-25 | 274 | +0.27 (+0.2) | 51% | -1.90 (-1.2) | yes | -0.25 (-0.2) |

**VERDICT: nothing** — pooled FOLLOW -0.69 bp, t -0.5, n 562, hit 50%. Gate: both halves > 0, pooled t ≥ 2.5, beats placebo both halves.

_Note: pair-months share the dollar side, so the three pairs are not fully independent — the pooled t is somewhat optimistic. Read the per-pair table._
