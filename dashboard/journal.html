Why the journal works

It stops you rewriting the story. Written before entry, the plan can't be adjusted to fit the result.

It finds your real edge. After 40 trades, “My journal” shows which setups, sessions and moods win. You may find your edge is only in London, or only when the quadrant agrees.

It separates early from wrong. The “where did price go after” question is the same test we ran on the bot (P73). Its losers were wrong, not early, so stop and entry tweaks wouldn't help. Your journal answers it for you.

It shows your leaks. Revenge or bored entries and moved stops show up as their own numbers.

Weekly review (Saturday, 15 min)

1. Win rate and average R this week vs the bot's.

2. Trades where you broke the plan: how did they do vs plan-followed trades?

3. Any trades with “Afraid to miss it”, “Want it back” or “Bored”? Their result?

4. Stop-outs: how many went on to the target (early) vs kept going (wrong)?

5. Overrides: helped or hurt, and were they on your rules list?

6. One rule to keep and one to change for next week. Write it in the next entry's thesis.

House rules

Max one manual trade per killzone.

No manual trade in the same dollar direction while the bot is in a trade. EURUSD, GBPUSD and NZDUSD are one dollar bet.

Bot and manual losses share one 6% daily limit. If it's hit, both stop.

Override the bot only for unscheduled news or a malfunction.

Decide after 40 trades, not 4. Fewer is luck.
